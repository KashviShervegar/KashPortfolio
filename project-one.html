Circles

An exploration of the circles present all around Manhattan which led to the creation of a fun and interactive spherical publication.

Inspired by the idea of children's books, it is full of imagery. The pages are thick & bound by velcro which allows you to rearrange the book in any manner.

This project also includes a website with a matching game which educates us of interesting NYC facts that relate to the observed and captured circles.
